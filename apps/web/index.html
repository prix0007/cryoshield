01 · The problem

Drives fail. Paper fades.

Thumb drives, notebooks and hard disks wear out, get lost, or burn. Most backups of a seed phrase are one bad day away from gone.

2036

02 · How it works

One tap. Sealed in your browser.

Tap your security key and it produces a secret only it can make, for this site only. Your browser uses it to lock your vault with AES-256-GCM before anything leaves your device. Only your keys can open it: CryoShield never sees your secrets or your keys.

Read the vault format

03 · Where it lives

Stored on-chain. Copied to Arweave.

The sealed vault, about 1 KB, is written to a public blockchain (OP Sepolia during this test phase), and a copy goes to Arweave’s permanent archive. Anyone can see the locked data. Only your keys can open it.

Only you can read it.

Your secrets are locked on your device before anything leaves it. The keys that open them live in the security keys you hold, never with us or anyone else.

How CryoShield compares with typical cloud storage
Typical cloud storage CryoShield
Who can read your data You, and the provider (who holds or can reset the keys) Only you
Account and password to lose or have hacked Yes None
Can be frozen, deleted or shut down Yes, by the provider No: stored on a public blockchain and Arweave
Works if the company disappears No Yes: the open-source recovery tool reads it directly

Some password managers also encrypt end to end. The difference is that CryoShield has no account, no servers holding your data, and no company you have to outlast.

04 · Resilience

Lose a key, not your vault.

You set up at least two keys, and any one of them opens your vault. Lost one? Open the vault with another and add a replacement.

If you lose every key, nobody can open the vault, not even us. There is no reset and no recovery service. Keep your keys in different places.

05 · Independence

Survives us too.

An open-source desktop recovery tool opens your vault with just your key. It reads the vault straight from the public blockchain or Arweave, so it keeps working even if this website and CryoShield disappear.

06 · Time

Built for decades.

Your vault stays readable as long as the blockchain or the Arweave copy exists, and at least one of your keys still works. Arweave is designed to pay for storage for at least 200 years. No one can promise any technology lasts that long, so keep your keys safe and check them now and then.

Free to use.

CryoShield pays the network fee each time your vault is saved, so you don’t need a crypto wallet or any cryptocurrency. You just need two security keys.

If fee sponsorship is ever paused, saving waits until it’s back. Opening your vault never depends on it.

Questions, answered.

What do I need?

Two FIDO2 security keys that support the PRF extension (hmac-secret), such as a YubiKey 5 with firmware 5.2 or newer, and a recent version of Chrome, Edge or Firefox. The app checks your browser before you start.

Can CryoShield read my secrets?

No. Your secrets are encrypted in your browser with a key that only your security keys can produce. CryoShield has no backend; this website only serves the app’s files, and never receives your secrets or your keys.

What if I lose a key?

Open your vault with any other enrolled key, then use “Add a key” to set up a replacement. That’s why you need at least two.

What if I lose all my keys?

Then nobody can open the vault: not you, not CryoShield, not the recovery tool. This is the cost of a backup that only you control.

Is it safe to use today?

CryoShield is a testnet preview on OP Sepolia and has not been independently audited. The code and the vault format are open source, and the encryption uses well-known open-source cryptography libraries (@noble) with published test vectors. Please keep your existing backups too.

How much can I store?

About 1 KB of text: plenty for seed phrases, 2FA backup codes and recovery codes, but not for files.

What happens if CryoShield shuts down?

Your vault stays on the public blockchain and on Arweave. The open-source desktop recovery tool can open it with your key without this website.
