About donations

  • Voluntary. CryoShield is free to use whether or not you donate.
  • No perks. A donation buys no goods, services, features or priority support.
  • Non-refundable. Blockchain transfers can’t be reversed, and we can’t return or recover funds, including anything sent on the wrong network.
  • What it funds: the network fees CryoShield sponsors, hosting, and an independent audit.
  • Who receives it: the open-source maintainer, personally. There is no company or charity, so there are no tax receipts.
  • Privacy: this page has no analytics, and we don’t track donations or link them to vaults. The blockchain itself is public: your sending address, the amount and the time are visible to anyone, permanently. See the privacy policy and terms.
